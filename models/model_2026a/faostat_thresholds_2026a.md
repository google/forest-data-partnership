# FAOSTAT Probability Thresholds for FDaP `model_2026a` Commodity Maps (2018–2024, ≥ 70% Area Coverage Adjusted)

> This is a **Coverage-Filtered & Overlap-Adjusted Dataset**
> (≥ 70% country area intersection with bounds `[-180, -24, 180, 24]`).
> This results in 780 `(commodity, year, country)` combinations across
> commodities {`cocoa`, `coffee`, `palm`, `rubber`}, years {`2018, 2020, 2022, 2024`},
> countries with `intersectionProportion >= 0.70`, and countries with applicable statistics
> in the FAOSTAT database. *Note*: Definitional mismatches can result in implausible thresholds.
> It is YOUR responsibility to set thresholds to levels that are appropriate for your use case.
>
> -   Countries with `< 70%` intersection area are excluded.
> -   Countries with partial overlap (70% ≤ `intersectionProportion` \< 100%)
>     are marked with **`*`**, and their target FAOSTAT harvested area is scaled
>     by `intersectionProportion`
>     (`adjusted_faostat_ha = faostat_ha * intersectionProportion`).
> -   **Combined Table Asset**:
>     `projects/forestdatapartnership/assets/faostat_thresholds_2026a`
> -   **Cocoa Table Asset**:
>     `projects/forestdatapartnership/assets/faostat_thresholds_2026a_cocoa`
> -   **Coffee Table Asset**:
